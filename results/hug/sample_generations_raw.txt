flour, sugar, butter, eggs, milk, creme fraiche, vanilla, egg yolk, granola cake, bittersweet chocolate, powdered sugar <|DIRECTIONS|> Lightly grease a 10-inch springform pan; set aside. In a medium bowl, combine melted butter, egg yolks, milk, vanilla, creme fraiche, and remaining sugar, stirring until smooth. Beat egg whites in a small bowl with mixer on high speed until stiff peaks form (1 to 2 minutes). Fold yolk into butter mixture until batter is smooth. Pour mixture into prepared pan. Bake in a 350 degree oven until top is set like pancake with faster baked cake, 18 to 20 minutes. Cool on a wire rack 5 minutes. In a small bowl, stir chocolate pieces together until smooth; spread onto a serving platter. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes. Place cake on top with a few slices of bittersweet chocolate. Sprinkle caramel on top of cake, and serve. <|EOS|>
